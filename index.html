Our mission

Three things, for people building for their own community.

Exposure

We put community projects in front of an audience. Videos, showcases, and posts across our channels — the reach a small team can't buy.

Support

Feedback, code review, and help getting unstuck. The parts of shipping that are hardest to do alone.

Community

A Discord of people building the same kind of thing at the same stage, in public, where asking a beginner question costs nothing.

FAQ

Questions we get.

What counts as community-centric software?

Software built for a group of people rather than a market. A tool for a club, a campus, a neighborhood, a game server, a nonprofit. If the users know each other, it counts.

Do I need to be experienced?

No. First projects and first-time builders are the point. Bring the thing you're not sure is good enough yet.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Big Things is a nonprofit — exposure and support are free to the people we work with.

Does the project have to be finished?

No. Work in progress is often the more interesting story, and showing it early is how you find the people who want to help.

Are you taking projects now?

Yes. The Discord is open and we're taking projects — bring whatever you're working on, however far along it is.

How can I support Big Things?

Join the Discord, share a project, or point someone toward us who's building one. Reach is the thing we're short on, and it's free to give.
